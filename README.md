### Curious about the science behind the visuals?

I'm a **Visiting Researcher at the University of Hertfordshire**, with an **MSc in Astrophysics with Advanced Research** and a **BTech in Electronics & Communication Engineering**. My work spans **precision radial-velocity instrumentation**, **feedback and active optics**, **exoplanet science** and **reproducible scientific software**.

*How do we turn an extremely faint signal into a trustworthy measurement?* That's the question connecting these projects.
