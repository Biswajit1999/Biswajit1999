### From our planet, looking outward

I'm a **Visiting Researcher at the University of Hertfordshire**, with an **MSc in Astrophysics with Advanced Research** and a background in **Electronics & Communication Engineering**. I explore the meeting point of **astronomical instruments, exoplanet discovery, optical measurements, and computational astronomy**.

*What can we learn about a world we may never visit—just by studying the light that reaches Earth?*

### Signal Lab

**Three ways of reading starlight.** Watch the simulated observations progress from a planet crossing a star, to absorption lines in a spectrum, to a star's tiny radial-velocity wobble.
