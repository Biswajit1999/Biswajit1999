### A little about my work

I'm a **Visiting Researcher at the University of Hertfordshire**, with an **MSc in Astrophysics with Advanced Research** and a **BTech in Electronics & Communication Engineering**. My research interests connect **high-precision radial velocity spectroscopy**, **active optics and closed-loop feedback**, **exoplanet science**, and **scientific software**.

The question that drives much of my work: ***how do we turn extremely small astrophysical signals into measurements we can actually trust?***
