## EPRV Spectrograph Landscape · v0.15.0

**Research question.** How strongly does resolving power improve the local photon-limited Doppler bound as intrinsic stellar line width changes?

**Generated result.** R=100,000 to 150,000 improves the controlled bound by 37.1%, 11.1%, and 2.48% for intrinsic widths of 1.0, 2.5, and 5.0 km/s; the broad-line null below 10% is not rejected.

**Boundary of inference.** Local synthetic photon-information experiment, not an end-to-end instrument performance forecast.

## ExoLight Transit Lab · v1.4.0

**Research question.** How badly can correlated time-series noise invalidate iid transit-depth uncertainties, and can event-clustered intervals restore coverage?

**Generated result.** At AR(1) rho=0.8, nominal 95% iid coverage falls to 54.9% and 56.1%; event-clustered intervals reach 93.2% and 92.1%.

**Boundary of inference.** Controlled noise-model experiment, not proof that every observed transit has AR(1) residuals or that one estimator solves all red-noise structure.

[Repository](https://github.com/Biswajit1999/exolight-transit-lab) · [Versioned release](https://github.com/Biswajit1999/exolight-transit-lab/releases/tag/v1.4.0) · [Live evidence](https://biswajit1999.github.io/exolight-transit-lab/) · source `3230f3a`

## EXOhSPEC Th-Ar Atlas · v0.3.0

**Research question.** Can a multi-exposure ladder preserve strong calibration-feature centroids better than one long exposure without degrading faint features?

**Generated result.** Across 7,000 strong-feature injections, pixelwise HDR lowers centroid RMSE from 0.012954 to 0.002086 pixel, an 83.9% reduction; faint-feature RMSE is unchanged.

**Boundary of inference.** Detector-domain simulation in pixels, not an atomic identification, wavelength solution, or measured radial-velocity precision.

## LATTICE Radiation Twin · v0.2.0

**Research question.** Can eight historical HST epochs distinguish calendar time from four cumulative radiation proxy components well enough to interpret individual coefficients?

**Generated result.** The attribution gate fails: two residual degrees of freedom, condition number 175.31, maximum VIF 4,881.4, and three coefficient sign changes under leave-one-epoch-out refits.

**Boundary of inference.** Post hoc predictor-separability diagnostic, not a radiation-damage estimate, causal claim, or authorization to open the sealed holdout.

## Finding Earth 2.0 · v2.1.2

**Research question.** When the objective is reducing planet-radius uncertainty, can a larger scalar stellar-radius score justify an indirect observation without measured covariance?

**Generated result.** Thirteen targets support both actions. Stellar radius has the larger own-parameter score for 10, but the indirect route wins for none at |rho| <= 0.90; Kepler-296 f needs |rho|=0.995225.

**Boundary of inference.** Correlation-grid sensitivity analysis, not a measured joint posterior or instrument-time recommendation; exposure cost is unmodelled.

## AstroBis Small-Body Evidence · v1.3.1

**Research question.** How sensitive is a 0.05 AU and 140 m encounter screen to distance intervals, missing diameters, assumed albedo, and row-limited coverage?

**Generated result.** In a reviewed 7,500-row JPL CAD prefix, 586 encounters are nominally close, 396 remain close at the three-sigma maximum, and 190 nominal-close rows cross the boundary.

**Boundary of inference.** Row-limited encounter-sensitivity audit, not MOID, PHA status, impact probability, Torino score, or hazard assessment.
